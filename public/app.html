What moves the score

Four weighted signals, each capped, summed and clamped to 100. Every score on the terminal renders this exact breakdown — no black box.

Unique wallet velocity

max +30

Distinct trader wallets seen in the last 10 seconds, ×1.2. A real crowd arriving beats one whale washing volume — sybils reuse wallets, crowds don't.

min(30, round(unique_wallets_10s × 1.2))

Buy / sell imbalance

max +30

Buy volume ÷ total volume over 60s, scaled by a confidence factor so one lucky buy on a fresh pool can't spike the score.

round(buy_ratio × 30 × min(1, trades_60s / 6))

Liquidity added (60s)

max +25

Fresh USD added to the pool in the last minute ÷ 400. Someone backing the pool while you watch it is the strongest conviction there is.

min(25, round(liq_added_60s / 400))

Volume momentum (60s)

max +15

USD volume rolling through the pool in the last minute ÷ 100 — the raw fuel under a launch.

min(15, round(vol_60s / 100))

State ladder

IDLE · score < 55 WATCH · 55 ≤ score < 75 DEEP · score ≥ 75 → alert + gRPC tx filter

Crossing into DEEP does three things at once: the row flashes, one latency-stamped alert fires (console → Discord/Telegram), and the pool's addresses are added to the Yellowstone gRPC transaction filter — so bandwidth only widens where conviction actually is. On reconnect the stream resumes with fromSlot = lastSeen + 1 and the server replays the gap.
